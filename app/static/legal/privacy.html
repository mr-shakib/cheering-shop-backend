1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number, profile photo and password. Passwords are stored only as a one-way hash; we cannot read them.
  • Delivery addresses: street address, apartment, landmark, city, postal code, a contact phone number, and the map location (latitude and longitude) you pin for delivery.
  • Orders: what you order, from which store, delivery instructions, the payment method you choose, and the payment status.
  • Messages and reviews: messages you send in an order's chat, and ratings or reviews you post.
  • Store and rider applications: if you apply to sell or deliver with us, the business and contact details you submit with the application.

Information created when you use the Service

  • Security and sign-in data: the IP address and device/browser description associated with each signed-in session, one-time verification codes, and the time you last signed in.
  • Biometric sign-in: if you turn it on, your device's fingerprint or face data never leaves your device. We store only a public key generated by your device, and a device identifier and name, so we can verify that a sign-in came from that device.
  • Two-factor authentication: if you enable it, we store the secret for your authenticator app in encrypted form.
  • Rider location: if you deliver with us, your device's location is collected while you are on shift, so orders can be assigned to nearby riders and customers can follow their delivery.

Payment information

We do not store card numbers or mobile wallet PINs. For cash on delivery we record only that the order was paid. If you pay online, the payment is processed by the payment provider, and we receive only the payment status and a transaction reference.

2. Sign in with Google

If you choose "Sign in with Google", Google shares the following with us, with your permission: your name, email address, profile photo, whether Google has verified the email address, and a Google account identifier. We request only the basic openid, email and profile permissions. We do not get access to your Gmail, contacts, Drive, calendar or any other Google data, and we never see your Google password.

We use this information only to create your Cheering Shop account or sign you in to it. If the email address already belongs to a Cheering Shop account, the two are linked so you keep one account and one order history.

Cheering Shop's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except as needed to provide the Service, for security, or to comply with the law.

You can remove Cheering Shop's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. This stops future sign-ins with Google but does not delete your Cheering Shop account; see section 7 for that.

3. How we use information

  • To create and manage your account and sign you in.
  • To take, prepare and deliver your orders, and to let you track them.
  • To let you, the store and the rider communicate about an order.
  • To send service emails, such as verification codes and password resets.
  • To keep the Service and your account secure, for example by detecting suspicious sign-ins, limiting repeated attempts and ending stolen sessions.
  • To show store ratings and reviews, and to improve the Service.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations and to resolve disputes.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Who we share it with

  • Stores receive the details of orders placed with them (items, instructions, and the name you ordered under).
  • Riders assigned to your order receive your name, contact phone number and delivery address so they can deliver it.
  • Service providers who run parts of the Service for us, under contract and only for that purpose: our cloud hosting provider (servers and databases), Cloudflare (storage of uploaded images such as photos), Resend (delivery of service emails), and Google (Sign in with Google).
  • Authorities, when we are required to by law, or when necessary to protect the rights, property or safety of our users, the public or Cheering Shop.
  • A successor, if Cheering Shop is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, in which case this policy continues to apply to your information.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account, address and profile information: while your account is open.
  • Orders, payment records and related messages: for as long as needed to provide the Service and to meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.
  • One-time verification codes: deleted automatically within two days of expiring.
  • Sign-in sessions: deleted automatically 30 days after they expire or are ended.

When we no longer need information, we delete it or anonymise it so it no longer identifies you.

6. Security

We protect your information with measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, encrypted two-factor secrets, short-lived access tokens, and limits on repeated sign-in attempts. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and enable two-factor authentication.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correct: you can view and edit your profile and addresses in the app at any time.
  • Remove addresses you no longer use, and turn biometric sign-in or two-factor authentication off, from the app.
  • Delete your account: email support@cheeringshop.online from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We will confirm the request and delete or anonymise your personal information, except for records we must keep by law (such as completed order and payment records).
  • Other requests: to ask for a copy of your information, or to object to how we use it, contact us using the details below.

8. Children

The Service is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Service changes. We will change the "Last updated" date above, and for significant changes we will notify you in the app or by email before they take effect.
