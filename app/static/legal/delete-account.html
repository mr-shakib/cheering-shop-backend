Delete your Cheering Shop account

Cheering Shop · Last updated 15 October 2026

You can delete your Cheering Shop account at any time. This applies to customer, store partner and rider accounts. Deleting is permanent and cannot be undone.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open the Cheering Shop app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. If your account has a password, you will be asked for it.

Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out on every device.

Without the app

Email support@cheeringshop.online with the subject "Delete my account". Send it from the email address on your account, or include the phone number on your account. We will confirm the request is yours, delete the account within 30 days, and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your name, email address, phone number and profile photo.
  • Your password, two-factor and biometric sign-in settings, and any link to your Google account.
  • Saved addresses, favourite stores, your cart, notifications and devices registered for push notifications.
  • Posts you made in the community.
  • For riders: identity documents, licence, date of birth, payout details and location history.
  • For store partners: your partner application, including identity documents and payout details. Your store is closed and removed from the app.

What is kept, and for how long

Some records must be kept for legal, tax and accounting reasons and to resolve disputes. We keep them for as long as those obligations require, without your name, email, phone or photo:

  • Orders and payment records, including the delivery address of each order.
  • Payouts made to store partners and riders.
  • Ratings and reviews you left, and messages about an order or a support request.

Store partners and riders: if we still owe you earnings, or a withdrawal is being processed, the account cannot be deleted until that money is paid out. Withdraw it in the app or contact us to settle it first. An account also cannot be deleted while one of its orders is still being prepared or delivered.

After deletion you can sign up again with the same email or phone number. It will be a new, empty account. More about how we handle your information is in our Privacy Policy.
